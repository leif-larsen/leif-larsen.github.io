# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

## Overview

www.leiflarsen.org is a static website. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics and does no tracking. I don't collect personal information about visitors.

## What happens when you visit

- **Hosting.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As the host, GitHub may process standard server logs, such as your IP address, when your browser requests a page. See the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Fonts.** Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the fonts load. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Theme preference.** If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.
- **Embedded video.** One older post embeds a YouTube video. Opening that post loads the player from YouTube, which may set its own cookies.

The share buttons and social icons are plain links. Nothing is sent to LinkedIn, X, Facebook or the others unless you click one.

## Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, I'll update this page and the date above.
